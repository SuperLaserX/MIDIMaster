# Access Virus kb

The Virus kb (2000, keyboard) runs the Virus B OS 4.9 (its readme: "OS4.90 is compatible with the
Virus b, kb and Indigo"; the OS's boot text for it is "VIRUS kb"). Its editor is the Virus b's
(`js/devices/defs/access-virus-kb.js` over `access-virus-abc.js` and the B OS tables): see
[access-virus-b.md](access-virus-b.md) and [access-virus-abc.md](access-virus-abc.md). The keyboard
settings of the global page (KEYBOARD, KEYB MODWHEEL / PEDAL / PRESSURE / VELOCITY menus) are the
ones its OS offers. Not checked on a Virus kb (none was connected).
