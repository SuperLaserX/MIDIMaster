# Access Virus Indigo 2

The Virus Indigo 2 (2003, keyboard) runs the Virus C OS (the update readme: OS 6.5 for the Virus c, kc
and Indigo-2; the 6.6 flash dump prints "I n d i g o  2" when IRAM 75h is 2 or more). Its editor is the
Virus C's (`js/devices/defs/access-virus-indigo2.js` over `access-virus-abc.js` and the C OS 6.6 tables):
see [access-virus-c.md](access-virus-c.md) and [access-virus-abc.md](access-virus-abc.md). The keyboard
settings of the global page are the ones its OS offers. Not checked on an Indigo 2 (none was connected).
