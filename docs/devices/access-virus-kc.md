# Access Virus KC

The Virus KC (2002, keyboard) runs the Virus C OS (the update readme: OS 6.5 for the Virus c, kc and
Indigo-2; the 6.6 flash dump prints "V I R U S    K C" when IRAM 75h is 1). Its editor is the Virus C's
(`js/devices/defs/access-virus-kc.js` over `access-virus-abc.js` and the C OS 6.6 tables): see
[access-virus-c.md](access-virus-c.md) and [access-virus-abc.md](access-virus-abc.md). The keyboard
settings of the global page (KEYBOARD, KEYBOARD MODE, KEYB MODWHEEL / PEDAL 1 / PEDAL 2 / PRESSURE /
VELOCITY menus) are the ones its OS offers. Not checked on a Virus KC (none was connected).
