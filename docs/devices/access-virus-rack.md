# Access Virus Rack

The Virus Rack (2000) is the Virus B's sound engine in a rack unit. No Rack OS is in the firmware
collection, so its editor (`js/devices/defs/access-virus-rack.js`) **assumes** the Virus B OS 4.9:
the same single, multi and global parameters, ranges and texts, the same SysEx and banks. Its own
smaller panel and menus are not mapped; a parameter the Rack lacks is simply ignored by it. See
[access-virus-b.md](access-virus-b.md) and [access-virus-abc.md](access-virus-abc.md). Not checked
on a Rack (none was connected). Open: a Rack OS image, to replace the assumption with its own tables.
